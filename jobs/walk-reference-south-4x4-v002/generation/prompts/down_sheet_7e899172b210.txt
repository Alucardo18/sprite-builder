SHEET GENERATION CONTRACT - HIGHEST PRIORITY
Create one complete native-resolution sprite sheet for a production 2D top-down action RPG.
This is one animation and one direction, not a single frame and not a contact-sheet preview.
Animation: walk. Direction: down.
Requested source canvas: 256 × 64 pixels.
Layout: horizontal; 1 row(s) × 4 column(s); frame cells are
read in deterministic order left-to-right. Approximate cell size: 64.0 ×
64.0 pixels. Gutter between cells: 0 pixels.
The sheet must contain exactly 4 animation cells and no extra poses.

PIXEL-ART STYLE CONTRACT
Authentic 16-bit-era top-down RPG pixel art with a compact limited palette, crisp hard-edged pixel clusters, deliberate 1 px and 2 px details at native scale, stepped silhouettes, and no anti-aliasing, painterly rendering, vector smoothness, 3D rendering, or modern high-resolution illustration..
Treat 16-bit as the visual design language, not PNG color depth. Use readable clusters, stable outline weight, restrained shading, and nearest-neighbor pixel logic..
Use one fixed elevated orthographic top-down camera, fully overhead/cenital or only slightly isometric. Keep the top plane of the head and shoulders visible, foreshorten vertical surfaces consistently, and never drift into eye-level front, profile, platformer, or side-scroller framing..
Use and simplify the character palette exclusively from the supplied concept image; do not borrow colors from the movement reference..
Use one stable light direction and value hierarchy across all four cells..

ANIMATION CONTINUITY CONTRACT
The movement reference contributes only movement, timing, poses, rhythm, and cell distribution. Generate a natural south/down walk cycle viewed from the elevated camera, with clear opposite arm-and-leg alternation, coherent contact and passing phases, hands changing sides between frames, and no repeated or frozen arm poses..
Readable walk pose; stable torso and ground plane; move only anatomy and equipment required by the named phase.
Show the complete character without cropping and do not add a shadow.

Preserve a stable ground-support row across frames. Legacy scale contract: keep body scale consistent with the approved character reference and require visual review against idle before integration.

The returned PNG itself is the authoritative source artifact; downstream code may only record
logical cell regions for an atlas.
