Create one complete native-resolution sprite sheet for a production 2D top-down action RPG.

Requested source canvas: 256 × 64 pixels.
Layout: horizontal; 1 row(s) × 4 column(s); frame cells are
read in deterministic order left-to-right. Approximate cell size: 64.0 ×
